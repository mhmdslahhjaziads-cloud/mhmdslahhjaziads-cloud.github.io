## Approved typography

Beiruti is used for headings and Tajawal for text and controls, following the approved pairing. The Arabic and Latin WOFF2 files are hosted locally. Tajawal uses 400, 500 and 700; Beiruti is restricted to the heading weight range 700–800 with FontTools. Source licenses are preserved in `assets/fonts/BEIRUTI-OFL.txt` and `assets/fonts/TAJAWAL-OFL.txt`. Sources: [Beiruti](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/beiruti), [Tajawal](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/tajawal).
